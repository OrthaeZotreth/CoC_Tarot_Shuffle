CARD XII
The Hanged Man
The Skyward Fall
"Up becomes down, and the sky becomes a bottomless pit."
I. The Hanged Man
Gravity Inversion • The Skyward Fall
⚔️ Real-Time (1d10 Rounds)
The investigator falls to their knees and frantically grips the nearest floor fixture or furniture leg, screaming that they are about to "fall into the sky."
⏳ Summary (1d10 Hours)
The investigator suffers intense vertigo and refuses to walk upright, crawling or insisting on being tied to heavy objects whenever indoors or under open sky.
📜 Keeper Mechanics
Movement speed reduced to crawling at 1/4 normal rate. Suffers +1 Penalty Die on all physical checks except clinging for safety. Cannot perform ranged or melee attacks.
